Meet Francine

A walking, talkingtestimonial.

Attorney. Author. Speaker. Georgetown Law graduate, marathon runner, mentor — and living proof that it is never too late to become who you were meant to be.

Hello, I'm Francine

I help people protect what they create — and become who they're meant to be.

For 35 years I've practiced law, helping authors, artists, entrepreneurs and families protect their creative work, their brands and their legacies through trademarks, copyrights, publishing agreements, wills and trusts.

But my most important qualification isn't on my diploma. I know what it's like to start with nothing — no self-esteem, no future anyone could see. What changed my life wasn't a pep talk or a mirror full of affirmations. It was action: small, often uncomfortable steps in the direction of the person I wanted to be.

That's what Esteemable Acts is about, and it's how I practice law, too — with honesty, preparation and genuine care for the people I serve.

J.D., Georgetown Law Admitted CA · NY · DC WealthCounsel Trailblazer Law school faculty Marathon runner

The journey

From the streets to the bar.

Francine shares her story openly — on stage, in her books and with clients — because someone out there needs to know that change is possible.

  1. The beginning

    Raised in poverty in the South Bronx

    By fourteen, Francine was addicted to heroin and alcohol. She dropped out of high school and ended up on the streets of New York, living what she would later call a life of addiction, crime and dysfunction.

  2. Rock bottom

    Told she would never walk again

    Struck by a car in her twenties, she was told she would never walk again. It was one of many moments that could have been the end of the story.

  3. The turning point

    A mentor named Louise

    Then she met Louise — a woman who got in her face and taught her that self-esteem isn't something you wait for. It's something you build, one right action at a time. Francine got clean and sober, and stayed that way.

  4. 1986

    Back to school

    The high-school dropout earned her bachelor's degree from Hunter College in New York.

  5. 1989

    Georgetown University Law Center

    She graduated from one of the country's top law schools with a Juris Doctor.

  6. 1991

    Admitted to practice

    On January 14, 1991, Francine began practicing law. She is admitted in New York, California and the District of Columbia, focusing on intellectual property and, later, estate planning.

  7. 1995

    Finding her voice on stage

    She began speaking professionally — to corporations, associations, recovery communities and bar groups — about self-esteem, fear and the law.

  8. 2003

    Esteemable Acts

    Her story and method became a book with a six-figure deal from a New York publisher: Esteemable Acts: 10 Actions for Building Real Self-Esteem (Broadway Books). 52 Weeks of Esteemable Acts (Hazelden) followed in 2005, and Staying Legal (ASTD Press) in 2007.

  9. 2021

    WealthCounsel Trailblazer Award

    Recognized by WealthCounsel for advancing her community through knowledge, excellence and education — a reminder, she wrote, that it's never too late to keep trying.

  10. Today

    Living life on purpose

    Francine practices from Palm Desert, California, with virtual appointments for clients everywhere she's licensed. She teaches intellectual property and contract drafting at the California Desert Trial Academy College of Law, writes her Life Pillars series, and still believes the next right action changes everything.
