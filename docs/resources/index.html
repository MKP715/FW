Vision & Values

Seven questions for an honest review.

Use these at the end of a year, the start of a season, or any Sunday night. Journal your answers — then choose one action.

  1. Take a snapshot of you: where are you right now in each area of your life — honestly?

  2. What are your top three values? Does your calendar reflect them? Does your bank statement?

  3. Where are your actions out of alignment with what you say matters?

  4. What one fear, if you walked through it this year, would change the most?

  5. What agreement with yourself have you been breaking? What would it take to keep it for seven days?

  6. Who could you be of service to this month?

  7. What will you do purely for joy?
