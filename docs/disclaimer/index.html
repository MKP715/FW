No attorney-client relationship

Reading this site, booking a consultation online, joining a Facebook community, asking for new-post emails, or contacting the office by phone, email or form does not create an attorney-client relationship. A relationship is formed only by a written engagement agreement. Until then, please don’t send confidential or time-sensitive information.

Permissions & reprints

Content on this website that belongs to Francine D. Ward or the Law Office of Francine D. Ward — including legal material, self-help material and personal development publications — may be reprinted, reproduced, duplicated or displayed only with written consent, regardless of the amount. In some cases a fee may be charged. To protect the integrity of the work, approved content must be reprinted or reproduced unedited and attributed in the exact manner we prescribe.

To request permission to use any content — articles, blog posts, essays, audio, images, photographs or any portion of them, in any format — email info@fwardattorney.com with “Permission request” in the subject line and include:

  • Your name
  • Your mailing address
  • Your phone number
  • The reason for, and nature of, the use — where and how the material will appear
  • The amount of material you’d like to use
  • The exact material to be reproduced (for example, the title and link of the article, or the passage itself)

Please allow approximately 10 business days for a reply after all of the required information has been received. Every effort is made to respond promptly and to accommodate special circumstances, but an immediate response can’t be guaranteed — so please plan ahead, request permission with ample lead time, and wait the full 10 business days before following up.

Privacy

Where a page includes a video, its preview image loads from YouTube’s image servers, and the video itself loads from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player only when you press play. Booking a consultation takes you to Calendly’s website, where Calendly’s privacy policy applies.

The Life Pillars worksheet saves your answers only in your own browser. If you use the contact or new-post email forms, your message is sent by email (or, if configured, through a form-processing service) solely so the office can respond to you. Your information is never sold.
