Short bio

Francine D. Ward is an intellectual property and estate planning attorney admitted in California, New York and the District of Columbia; the author of Esteemable Acts: 10 Actions for Building Real Self-Esteem (Broadway Books) and 52 Weeks of Esteemable Acts (Hazelden); and a professional speaker since 1995. A former high-school dropout who overcame addiction, she graduated from Georgetown University Law Center in 1989.

Long bio

Francine D. Ward is living proof that it's never too late to become who you were meant to be. At fourteen she was addicted to heroin and alcohol; by eighteen she was a high-school dropout on the streets of New York. With the help of a mentor who taught her that self-esteem comes from action, she got clean and sober, earned her bachelor's degree from Hunter College, and graduated from Georgetown University Law Center in 1989.

Francine has practiced law since January 14, 1991. Admitted in California, New York and the District of Columbia, she helps authors, artists, entrepreneurs and families protect their creative work, brands and legacies through trademarks, copyrights, publishing and licensing agreements, wills and trusts. She teaches intellectual property and contract drafting at the California Desert Trial Academy College of Law and received the WealthCounsel Trailblazer Award.

She is the author of Esteemable Acts: 10 Actions for Building Real Self-Esteem (Broadway Books), 52 Weeks of Esteemable Acts: A Guide to Right Living (Hazelden) and Staying Legal: A Guide to Copyright and Trademark Use (ASTD Press). A professional speaker since 1995, she has been featured on KPIX, KRON and ABC7 and in the ABA Journal, the Chicago Tribune and The Washington Post.
