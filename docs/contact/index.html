Good to know

Frequently asked

How do I book a consultation?

The quickest way is to choose a time online — the booking page lists the 30-minute consultation times that are open. You can also call 760-636-0066, email info@fwardattorney.com, or send a message with the form on this page.

Do I have to come into an office?

No. All appointments are virtual, so you can meet from your home, office or studio. If you’d prefer a phone call, just say so when you reach out.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The initial consultation is complimentary, by appointment. It’s a chance to explain what you need, ask questions and decide together whether working with the office is a good fit.

Which jurisdictions do you cover?

Francine is admitted to practice in California, New York and the District of Columbia. Trademark and copyright matters are largely federal, so in some cases help is possible beyond those places — ask during your consultation.

What should I bring to the consultation?

For IP matters: the name, logo or work you want to protect, how and where you use it, and any contracts you’ve been asked to sign. For estate planning: a rough list of assets, who you’d like to provide for, and any existing wills or trusts. Francine’s guide to creating an estate planning inventory (PDF) is a good place to start.

Can I send documents through this form?

Please don’t send confidential information until an attorney-client relationship has been established. The consultation is the right time to decide what to share and how.

Where can I learn more before we talk?

Browse the plain-English guides and the legal blog on the Insights page. If you’re still gathering information, you’re welcome to join Francine’s Legal Insights community on Facebook for ongoing guidance and support.

How do I book Francine to speak?

Choose “Speaking engagement” in the form and include your event date, audience and format. The speaking page has topics and a media kit.
