Questions

Before you call.

Do I need a lawyer to file a trademark?

You can file on your own, but many applications stumble on clearance, the description of goods and services, or specimens — and a refusal can cost you months and fees. A short consultation before filing is usually the best investment you can make in a brand.

When should I register a copyright?

Copyright exists when your work is created, but registration is generally required before you can sue for infringement in the U.S. — and registering early (before infringement, or within three months of publication) preserves statutory damages and attorney's fees.

Can you review a contract I've already been sent?

Yes. Publishing, licensing, collaboration, influencer and freelance agreements are reviewed with an eye to the clauses that matter most: rights granted, payment, term, termination and reversion.

Do you handle estate planning outside California?

Estate planning depends on state law. Francine is admitted in California, New York and the District of Columbia; ask during your consultation whether your situation is a fit.
